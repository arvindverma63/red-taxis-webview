## Description (1062/4000)
Book a First Taxis ride in seconds.

Set your pickup and destination, choose your vehicle and see a guaranteed fixed fare before you confirm. No surge pricing, no surprises.

WHY RIDERS CHOOSE FIRST TAXIS
- Fixed, transparent fares shown up front
- Licensed and verified local drivers
- Ride now, or schedule a taxi for later
- Track your driver live on the map
- Use your current location, search an address, or drop a pin on the map
- Save your favourite places for one-tap booking
- Pay by cash or card, with an official digital receipt for every trip
- Choose from saloon, estate and larger vehicles for extra passengers or luggage
- Local dispatch support, 24/7

HOW IT WORKS
1. Enter where you are and where you want to go.
2. Pick a vehicle and choose Now or a time that suits you.
3. Review your fare, confirm, and follow your driver to your door.

Your trip history, receipts and saved places are always in the app. Location is used only to set your pickup point, calculate your fare and show your driver's arrival.
