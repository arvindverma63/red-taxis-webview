Slide 4 • Step 3 Fleet Switching

Changing Companies from Settings

If you drive for multiple fleets or change taxi companies, you don't need to delete the app.

settings
1. Open Settings Menu Scroll down to the Fleet & Company section.
verified
2. View Current Status Shows current fleet (First Taxis) with a green Verified & Active badge.
sync
3. Tap "Change Taxi Company" Enter the new company ID & key (or scan a new QR code). The app switches instantly!

What if I enter the wrong Company ID? Tap the small [Change] button on the login screen to fix it immediately.
